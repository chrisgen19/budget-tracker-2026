Bookkeeping

Two problems that belong to the app, not the accounting

Unlabelled spending is the app's doing. ₱31,913 sits unlabelled across just four transactions — all of them bill payments — against ₱27,557 across forty-six manual entries. Payments created through the bill route skip label auto-apply, so the largest and most predictable expenses are the ones missing from every label breakdown.

Descriptions are fragmenting. One purchase is stored four different ways. A trailing space splits the main income source in two, and both a straight and a curly apostrophe appear in the same salary line. This matters more than it used to: the Telegram bot searches by description text, so one spelling silently misses the others.
